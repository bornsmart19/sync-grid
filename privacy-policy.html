Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Welcome to SyncGrid. SyncGrid is a global world clock and international meeting planner designed to help users compare time zones, local times, working hours, and potential meeting times.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use SyncGrid, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

SyncGrid is designed to work without requiring users to create an account.

Depending on how you use the website, SyncGrid may process information such as selected cities, time zones, meeting preferences, and settings entered into the website.

We do not intentionally ask users to provide sensitive personal information through the main SyncGrid dashboard.

2. Information Stored in Your Browser

Some SyncGrid features may use browser storage, such as local storage, to remember preferences or settings on your device.

Information stored locally by the website is intended to help provide a more convenient experience and is not necessarily transmitted to SyncGrid servers.

3. Cookies and Advertising

SyncGrid may use third-party advertising services in the future, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements on the website.

If advertising services are enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to provide, measure, and improve advertising and related services.

Google explains how information may be processed when websites use Google advertising and analytics services.

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

When required, SyncGrid may use Google's privacy and consent tools to provide users with choices regarding advertising and data processing.

4. Third-Party Services

SyncGrid may use third-party services for purposes such as website hosting, security, analytics, advertising, or other website functionality.

These third parties may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Analytics

If analytics services are introduced, they may collect information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and general usage information.

Analytics are used to understand how SyncGrid is used and to improve the website.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • operate and maintain SyncGrid;
  • improve website functionality;
  • remember user preferences;
  • understand website usage;
  • maintain security and prevent abuse;
  • provide advertising and measure advertising performance, where applicable.

7. Data Sharing

SyncGrid does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate the website, provide advertising, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

SyncGrid is intended to be a general-purpose website and is not designed specifically for children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the main website.

9. Your Choices

Depending on the services enabled on SyncGrid and your location, you may have choices regarding cookies, personalized advertising, analytics, and other forms of data processing.

You may also control or delete browser storage through your browser settings.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the website and information processed through it. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SyncGrid's features, services, advertising providers, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SyncGrid's privacy practices, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
